# Bayesian DNS literature evidence matrix

## Scope and evidence standard

This matrix separates results directly supported by a source from methodological analogies and project-specific choices. Page numbers below refer to the PDF version actually inspected. The 2007 NBER paper by Diebold, Li, and Yue and the 2010 SFB paper by Hautsch and Yang are the working-paper versions reviewed; the later journal metadata are identified separately. No quotations from an uninspected journal version are attributed to its working paper.

## What the sources do not justify

The numerical scales \(s_{\mu,j}=2s_{j,\mathrm{train}}\), \(s_{b,j}=0.03\), the log-variance prior SD of one, the rate-one truncated-exponential \(v_j\) prior, and the bound \(U=4\) are project-specific empirical-Bayes or regularization choices. They are justified, if at all, by the documented initial-training calibration, mathematical argument, and training-only prior-predictive audits—not by the cited literature. The revised numerical choices were introduced after the earlier specification had been evaluated; they were selected without using evaluation-period scores.

The \(\mathrm{Beta}(20,1.5)\) prior is imposed on a weekly transition coefficient in this study. Its implications must therefore be reported per week; importing the prior family does not amount to a frequency adjustment or evidence that Canadian yield-factor volatility has the same persistence as the source applications.

- The reviewed Diebold–Li–Yue source is *Global Yield Curve Dynamics and Interactions: A Dynamic Nelson–Siegel Approach*, NBER Working Paper 13588 (November 2007), available at <https://www.nber.org/papers/w13588>. It was later published in the *Journal of Econometrics* (2008), 146(2), 351–363, DOI 10.1016/j.jeconom.2008.08.017. The manuscript cites the 2007 working-paper record to match the inspected text.
- The reviewed Hautsch–Yang source is the January 2010 SFB 649 Discussion Paper 2010-004. The related 2012 journal article is already in `references.bib`; the working-paper entry is added so the inspected version is also cited.
- Kim et al. (1998) metadata and DOI are recorded in `references.bib`. The inspected PDF was an author-hosted copy of the published article.
- Omori et al. (2007) metadata and DOI are recorded in `references.bib`; the inspected source is the complete 2005 author manuscript, not a line-by-line review of the typeset journal article.
